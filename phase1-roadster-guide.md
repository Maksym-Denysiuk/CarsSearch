# 2024–2026 Roadster Buyer's Guide (Portugal / EU) — Phase 1: Discovery & Pricing

**Scope:** Roadsters (2+ world-known open-top two-seat sports cars, plus closely related open-top two-seaters), model years 2024–2026, buyer based in Portugal. Budget unconstrained at this stage.

**TL;DR**
- There are roughly two dozen "world-known" open-top two-seat sports cars available new or nearly-new in 2024–2026 model years, spanning from the Mazda MX-5 and Caterham Seven (both around €29,000–30,000 net) up to the ~€480,000 Ferrari SF90 Spider; the mainstream sweet spot for a genuine roadster is the MX-5, Caterham Seven, BMW Z4 and the electric MG Cyberster.
- All prices below are expressed as **pre-tax net figures (EUR, VAT removed)** and exclude Portuguese registration tax (ISV) and any import extras; because manufacturers rarely publish true ex-works prices, figures are derived from national list prices (German incl. 19% VAT, UK incl. 20% VAT, Italian incl. 22% VAT) or US MSRP (already tax-exclusive), with the basis flagged per model.
- Several models the user listed are NOT currently buyable new and are excluded: Audi R8 Spyder (final car built 22 March 2024, no successor), Lamborghini Huracán Spyder (replaced by the so-far coupe-only Temerario), Fiat 124 Spider (discontinued), Tesla Roadster (not yet in production), Rolls-Royce Dawn (discontinued, no current RR convertible), and the Mercedes-AMG GT Roadster (the R232 SL is its replacement). Alpine A110 and Lotus Emira are coupés, not roadsters.

## Key Findings

- **Definition applied:** true two-seat open-top sports cars plus closely related open-top two-seaters. The Fiat 500e Cabrio and MINI Convertible are 2+2 city convertibles (not strict two-seat roadsters) but are included per the user's request and flagged. Grand-tourer convertibles (Bentley Continental GTC, Ferrari 12Cilindri Spider) are 2+2 but included as world-known open-tops.
- **Pricing methodology:** Portugal retail prices bundle 23% VAT plus ISV registration tax, so they overstate the "pre-tax base." The cleanest EU proxy is the German or Italian manufacturer list price (which includes national VAT but no registration tax); dividing by the VAT factor approximates the ex-works+margin figure. Where only US MSRP exists, that figure is already tax-exclusive and is converted at roughly USD→EUR 0.92; where only UK pricing exists, VAT (20%) is removed and GBP→EUR converted at roughly 1.17. Every conversion is an approximation and flagged.
- **The euro entry point** is genuinely low: the Mazda MX-5 and Caterham Seven 170 both sit around €29,000–30,000 net, while the BMW Z4 is the mainstream "premium" roadster at roughly €44,000 net average.
- **Electric roadsters** remain a thin field: the MG Cyberster is the only mainstream new electric two-seat convertible, and its EU price has been inflated by an EU anti-subsidy tariff of 35.3% on China-built SAIC/MG cars (on top of the standard 10% import duty). [Electrive](https://www.electrive.com/2025/08/18/byd-and-mg-sidestep-eu-tariffs-with-plug-in-hybrids/)

## Details

**Affordable / mainstream roadsters.** The **Mazda MX-5** remains the definitive affordable roadster — the current ND (freshened for the 2024 model year with new LED lighting and a DSC-Track mode) starts in Portugal from **€36,673.70 including all taxes**, which nets to roughly €29,800 after removing 23% VAT; the RF retractable-hardtop opens from about €38,542 in Portugal. Note that **the 2024 MX-5 reached Portugal with only the 1.5 Skyactiv-G 132 PS engine — the 2.0-litre 184 PS remains available in other EU markets but not in Portugal.** The **Caterham Seven** spans an enormous range: the entry Seven 170 is £29,490 UK OTR, the 360 is £38,490, the 420 £41,990 (or £56,490 for the 420 Cup track special), [Top Gear](https://www.topgear.com/car-reviews/caterham/seven/buying) and the 620 £54,990 [Top Gear](https://www.topgear.com/car-reviews/caterham/seven/buying) — making Caterham both the cheapest way into a new roadster and, in top trim, a mid-price choice. The **BMW Z4 (G29)** is the mainstream premium pick, with German list prices from €42,900 (sDrive20i) to €63,100 (M40i); [AutoScout24](https://www.autoscout24.de/auto/bmw/bmw-z-reihe/bmw-z4/) the M40i's "Pure Impulse" package reintroduces a six-speed manual.

**Electric and city open-tops.** The **MG Cyberster** is currently the only mainstream new electric two-seat roadster, starting around €69,900 including VAT (Ireland). Its EU pricing has been hit by an **EU anti-subsidy tariff of 35.3% on MG (SAIC), levied on top of the standard 10% import duty**, [Electrive](https://www.electrive.com/2025/08/18/byd-and-mg-sidestep-eu-tariffs-with-plug-in-hybrids/) pushing German prices toward €80,000 including VAT. [MG EVs](https://www.mgevs.com/threads/the-cyberster-and-the-eus-punitive-tariffs.19225/) The **Fiat 500e Cabrio** (~€37,700 incl. VAT new) and **MINI Convertible** are 2+2 city convertibles rather than true two-seat roadsters, included here for completeness.

**Premium sports convertibles.** The **Porsche 718 Boxster** is a pivotal case: Porsche's product communications confirmed that production of all 982-generation 718 Boxster/Cayman variants (including RS models) ended in October 2025, and the internal-combustion 718 had already been withdrawn from EU markets from July 2024 due to EU General Safety Regulation (cybersecurity) rules. Late in 2025 only the range-topping 718 Spyder RS remained listed on the German configurator (€158,700 incl. VAT); [Porsche](https://configurator.porsche.com/de-DE/mode/model/982890) an all-electric 718 successor is expected. The **Chevrolet Corvette Stingray Convertible (C8)** is exceptional value on paper (US MSRP $76,995) but carries a large premium once privately imported and taxed into the EU. The **Mercedes-AMG SL (R232)** is the roadster flagship of the three-pointed star and effectively replaces the old AMG GT Roadster; the current AMG GT (C192) is coupe-only. The **Porsche 911 Cabriolet/Targa (992.2)** spans Carrera (US $133,400 Cabriolet) through the T-Hybrid GTS.

**Exotic / luxury open-tops.** The upper tier includes the **Aston Martin Vantage Roadster** (UK from ~£185,000) and **DB12 Volante** (US MSRP ~$288,500), **Maserati MC20 Cielo** (Italy €271,020 incl. VAT) and **GranCabrio**, **Ferrari 296 GTS, 12Cilindri Spider (Italy €435,000 incl. VAT) and SF90 Spider**, **McLaren Artura Spider and 750S Spider (US $392,000)**, and the **Bentley Continental GTC Speed** grand tourer (US $332,200). The **Ferrari SF90 Spider** is at the end of its production run (2020–2025), with EU used examples from about €468,800. The **Lamborghini Temerario** (Huracán successor) has launched as a coupe at a US base of $357,621; a Spyder/roadster variant has not yet been revealed, and the Huracán Spyder is discontinued.

**Explicitly excluded (not buyable new in 2024–2026):**
- **Audi R8 Spyder** — the final R8 was built at Neckarsulm's Böllinger Höfe on 22 March 2024 after 45,949 units; no successor is on sale (a future electric R8 is speculative only).
- **Lamborghini Huracán Spyder** — discontinued; replaced by the (coupe-only, so far) Temerario.
- **Fiat 124 Spider** — discontinued (2019–2020).
- **Tesla Roadster** — announced but not yet in production.
- **Rolls-Royce Dawn / Wraith** — order books closed; the electric Spectre is a coupe, so Rolls-Royce currently offers no convertible.
- **Mercedes-AMG GT Roadster** — no convertible in the current C192 generation; the SL R232 is its replacement.
- **Alpine A110** — two-seat mid-engine **coupé** (base ~€66,900 France incl. VAT), [Autodevot](https://www.autodevot.com/2025/11/alpine-a110-r-ultime-debuts-ultimate-price-tag/) not a convertible.
- **Lotus Emira** — mid-engine **coupé** (base ~€95,995 Germany incl. VAT), [GTspirit](https://gtspirit.com/2021/09/22/this-is-how-much-the-new-lotus-emira-sports-car-will-cost/) not a convertible.

## Recommendations

1. **If the goal is a pure, world-known roadster on a sensible budget:** shortlist the **Mazda MX-5 (ND)** first — it is the benchmark, cheapest to run, holds value well, and a lightly-used 2024–2025 example in Portugal (~€27,500–28,750 seen in dealer listings) is the single best value. Note that in Portugal you can only buy it with the 1.5 132 PS engine new. Threshold to change this: if you want more power/refinement and can roughly double the budget, step to the **BMW Z4 M40i**.
2. **If you want electric open-top motoring:** the **MG Cyberster** is currently the only mainstream option — but weigh the EU tariff situation carefully, because it materially changes the price. Threshold: if the delivered EU price stays under ~€70,000 incl. taxes, it is compelling; above ~€80,000 it competes with far more established premium roadsters and its value case weakens.
3. **If budget is €70,000–150,000 net:** the **Porsche 718 Boxster/Spyder RS** (while remaining EU stock lasts — act quickly, production has ended), **Mercedes-AMG SL 43/55**, and **Porsche 911 Carrera Cabriolet** are the class benchmarks.
4. **For exotics (€180,000+ net):** prioritise by drivetrain preference — hybrid/turbo V6 (Ferrari 296 GTS, McLaren Artura Spider), V8 (Aston DB12 Volante, McLaren 750S Spider, Maserati MC20 Cielo), or V12 grand touring (Ferrari 12Cilindri Spider, Bentley GTC). For a Phase 2 purchase, confirm EU-delivered pricing directly, as options routinely add 20–40%.
5. **Phase 2 next steps:** obtain official Portuguese/EU delivered quotes (which will include ISV registration tax and 23% VAT), since the ex-works figures here will rise substantially once Portuguese taxes are applied — particularly for high-CO₂ combustion exotics, where ISV is punishing. Use the net figures here only for cross-market comparison, not as expected Portuguese purchase prices.

## Caveats

- **All figures are pre-tax net approximations.** They exclude Portuguese ISV registration tax and 23% VAT and will be considerably lower than real Portuguese on-the-road prices. Combustion-engine and high-CO₂ models attract heavy ISV in Portugal.
- **Currency and basis vary by model.** Where only US MSRP was available it is tax-exclusive but reflects the US market, not EU pricing; EU list prices for these models are typically higher. GBP and USD conversions use approximate rates (GBP→EUR ~1.17, USD→EUR ~0.92) as of mid-2026.
- **Exotic "base" prices are largely theoretical.** Ferrari, Lamborghini, McLaren, Aston Martin and Bentley transaction prices routinely exceed the base by tens or hundreds of thousands once options are specified (e.g. a Ferrari Roma Spider tips over £220k with modest options).
- **Manufacturers do not publish true ex-works prices**, so several figures (Ferrari, Maserati, Alpine, Lotus, MINI, Cyberster net) come from dealer/press aggregators rather than official pricing pages and are flagged as estimates.
- **Availability is time-sensitive.** The Porsche 718 (982) ceased production in October 2025 [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porsche_Boxster_and_Cayman) and the ICE version left EU markets in July 2024; the Ferrari SF90 Spider is ending; the Lamborghini Temerario roadster is not yet available; the Audi R8 Spyder ended in March 2024. Confirm current orderability in Phase 2.
