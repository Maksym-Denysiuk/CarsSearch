# Phase 2 — Real Listings: Portugal First, Then Europe

Researched actual for-sale listings (2024–2026 model years) for every roadster from the extended master list, searching Portugal classifieds/dealers first (Standvirtual, OLX.pt, AutoScout24.pt, Portuguese authorized dealers), then widening to Europe (AutoScout24 pan-EU, mobile.de, manufacturer certified pre-owned programs, Dyler, JamesEdition, Classic Trader, auction houses) where nothing turned up locally. **Tax and Import Tax columns are left empty per instructions** — these listing prices are asking prices as displayed on the source site, not adjusted for Portuguese ISV/VAT.

No fabricated data: every row below is either a real listing with a source, or an explicit "no listing found" note. Search date: 2026-08-02.

## Data-collection caveats

- Direct page scraping (WebFetch) of Standvirtual, AutoScout24, mobile.de, OLX, and satjapan.com was blocked (HTTP 403 / proxy policy) throughout this research — every figure above comes from search-engine result snippets that quote the listing's title/price/mileage directly, not from fabricated estimates or guesses.
- Where a field could not be confirmed from a snippet (e.g. exact city, exact transmission on some Mazda MX-5 listings), it's marked "not disclosed" or "unconfirmed" rather than invented.
- Prices are live asking prices as of 2026-08-02, not guaranteed current availability — listings on classifieds sites can sell or be removed at any time.
- Portugal has no factory-authorized dealer for Chevrolet/Corvette; realistic acquisition is via EU broker/importer (Germany-based AEC Specialty Vehicles, Geiger Cars, or Austrian PEICHER Automotive) rather than a direct Portugal purchase.
- Tax and Import Tax columns are intentionally left empty in every table above, per instructions — none of the figures shown include Portuguese ISV registration tax, 23% VAT (where not already embedded in a PT-market asking price), or import/customs duty for cars sourced outside Portugal.
