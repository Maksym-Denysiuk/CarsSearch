# Roadster Master List — Extended Deep Research (converged)

**Supersedes/extends `phase1-roadster-guide.md`.** Ran iterative web research across mainstream, premium, grand-tourer, exotic/hypercar, EV, Asian-market, and boutique/kit-car segments (~30 search rounds) until additional searches stopped surfacing new *buyable* roadster models — the last two rounds returned only cars to exclude (fixed-roof coupés like the Apollo IE, Radford Type 62-2, Hispano Suiza Carmen Boulogne) or refinements of already-found models. Convergence reached 2026-08-02.

**Definition unchanged:** two-seat open-top sports cars (roadster/spider/speedster/roof-optional), plus closely related open-top two-seaters and 2+2 GT convertibles included for completeness and flagged. Model years 2024–2026. All prices pre-tax net EUR (VAT removed), excluding Portuguese ISV and import extras — same methodology as Phase 1 (basis flagged per model: DE/UK/IT list or US MSRP).

## What's new vs. the original Phase 1 table

- **Ferrari Roma Spider** — a genuine miss from the first pass (V8 GT convertible, in production since 2023, continuing 2024–2026).
- **Ultra-exotic/hypercar roadsters**, mostly sold-out limited runs, not previously covered: **Bugatti W16 Mistral**, **Gordon Murray T.33 Spider**, **Pagani Utopia Roadster**, **Lamborghini Fenomeno Roadster**, **Pininfarina B95 Speedster**.
- **New/updated models**: **Aston Martin Vanquish Volante** (new V12 flagship convertible), **Morgan Supersport** (replaces Plus Six), **AC Cobra GT Roadster** (relaunched AC Cars), **Maserati MCPura Cielo** (2026 rename/refresh of MC20 Cielo), **Wiesmann Thunderball** (electric roadster, in production since 2024 — missed first pass), **Mitsuoka Himiko** (MX-5-based Japanese boutique retro roadster, still made in tiny numbers).
- **Niche/kit-car tier added**: Caterham (already had), **Factory Five Mk5 Roadster** (US kit car), **VUHL 05** (Mexican), **Donkervoort P24 RS** (Dutch, 2026 production start), **Longbow Speedster** (new British EV), plus open-cockpit trackday cars (**Ariel Atom/Nomad**, **Radical SR3**, **KTM X-Bow**) flagged as borderline (no foldable/removable full roof in the traditional sense).
- **Upcoming, not yet orderable/deliverable** (tracked but excluded from Phase 2 purchase search): **Lamborghini Revuelto Roadster** (expected early 2026), **Lamborghini Temerario Roadster** (replaces Huracán Spyder, not yet launched), **Audi Concept C** production version (2027), **Tesla Roadster** (production pushed to 2027), **Polestar 6** (2026 production, reportedly deprioritized as of May 2026 per company statements), **BYD Formula X** and **Denza Z** (Chinese EV convertibles, production/EU availability not yet confirmed).
- **Important status change**: **BMW Z4** production ended March–May 2026 (Final Edition) — no successor announced. Still squarely in scope as a 2024–2026 model-year used/nearly-new car for Phase 2.
- **Confirmed excluded as fixed-roof coupés** (checked, not roadsters): Apollo IE, Radford Type 62-2, Hispano Suiza Carmen/Carmen Boulogne, Lamborghini Temerario (coupe, pending Roadster), Alpine A110, Lotus Emira.

## Caveats (deep-research pass)

- **No claim of absolute exhaustiveness for ultra-bespoke coachbuilders.** One-off or sub-10-unit commission cars (beyond those found) may exist without meaningful press coverage; this list captures every "world-known" model surfaced across ~30 search queries spanning mainstream, exotic, EV, Asian-market, and kit-car categories.
- **Sold-out limited editions** (Bugatti Mistral, Pagani Utopia Roadster, GMA T.33 Spider, Lamborghini Fenomeno Roadster, Pininfarina B95) have **no factory-new order path** — any Phase 2 acquisition would be secondary-market/auction only, at prices likely well above the original net figures shown.
- **Several models are not yet orderable** in the 2024–2026 window (Revuelto/Temerario Roadster, Audi Concept C, Tesla Roadster, Polestar 6, BYD Formula X, Denza Z) — tracked here for completeness but **excluded from Phase 2** purchase research until they reach market.
- Pricing conversions retain the same approximations as Phase 1 (GBP→EUR ~1.17, USD→EUR ~0.92, VAT removal by national rate).
